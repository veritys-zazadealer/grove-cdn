Scholar Grove — Practice and Lessons for Every Student

Scholar Grove is a free study library for students, teachers, and lifelong learners, with structured lessons and practice across every core subject.

Subjects

Mathematics, algebra, geometry, calculus, statistics, biology, chemistry, physics, English, reading comprehension, writing, grammar, literature, social studies, history, geography, Spanish, and computer science.

Grade Levels

Leveled lessons for elementary (K-5), middle school (6-8), high school (9-12), and college, each with reading passages, comprehension questions, and guided practice.

Test Prep

Dedicated practice for the SAT, ACT, AP exams, state assessments, TOEFL, and IELTS, with rubric-scored sample responses.

Study Tools

Interactive quizzes, flashcards, a focus timer, vocabulary builders, printable worksheets, and progress tracking for self-paced learning.
